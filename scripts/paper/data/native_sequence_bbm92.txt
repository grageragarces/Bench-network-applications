BBM92 written directly on SeQUeNCe 1.0.0, against qnetbench's engine at the same link fidelity.
Two routers 1 km apart, 1 ms classical delay, decoherence disabled, 32 seeds per row.
QBER = rounds breaking the Bell-pair correlation / rounds kept after sifting. The closed form for a Werner
pair at fidelity F is 2 (1 - F) / 3.

The native protocol, including the SeQUeNCe reservation that generates the pairs, takes 63 lines
of code. In qnetbench the same experiment is one TenantSpec, and it runs on all three supplies.
